    🏷️ Badges you'll see on moments

    TSD · Top Shot DebutThe player's first moment on Top Shot. Vince's TSD is his final career shot, Mar 11, 2020.
    RY · Rookie YearA moment from the player's rookie season.
    RP · Rookie PremiereFrom the player's first game in the league.
    RM · Rookie MintMinted while the player was a rookie.
    CY · Championship YearFrom a season the player's team won the title.
    🔒 LockedThe owner locked it for a while: it can't be sold until the lock ends.

    Badge rules can change. Check a moment's badge info on Top Shot for the exact meaning.
